How it works

  1. 1

    EMS uploads the patient

    From the field, EMS crews record patient details, vital signs, and treatment notes as they go.

  2. 2

    The hospital sees it live

    The receiving hospital's team sees the patient the moment it's uploaded — along with the ambulance's live position, a moving map marker, and a real-time ETA.

  3. 3

    Care teams prep in advance

    Vitals, treatment given, and estimated arrival time are all visible before the patient is at the door.

Built for the handoff
